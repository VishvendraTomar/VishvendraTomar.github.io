PRODUCTION BACKEND / 01

Workforce infrastructure.

Modernising the backend behind workforce scheduling, compliance and payments.

EXPLORE THE BREAKDOWN ↓
01 /

The context.

A workforce platform needs to coordinate worker assignments, declarations, wages and invoices while preserving business rules across time-sensitive changes. Legacy serverless workflows made separation of responsibilities, retries and consistency harder to reason about.

MY FOCUS

Worked as the sole backend engineer on a major modernization effort, designing modular FastAPI services, an expanded PostgreSQL domain schema and asynchronous business workflows.

WHAT WAS DELIVERED

Migrated more than 50 Cloud Functions to FastAPI; designed a 46-table PostgreSQL schema. The FastAPI service layer went into production with the existing Firestore database; the proposed PostgreSQL data migration was not completed during this phase.

02 /

Engineering decisions.

01

Separate business rules

Reworked function-based paths into service-oriented FastAPI routes, making validations, dependencies and execution boundaries clearer.

02

Design the data model

Modelled shifts, assignments, payments and linked entities in a 46-table PostgreSQL schema with transaction and access-control considerations. This was design work, not a claim of a fully completed production data migration.

03

Build for failure

Worked on background processing for external compliance requests, retries, queue separation and reconciliation so slow or failed integrations could be tracked.

03 /

What matters.

A migration is successful only when the new architecture preserves the real business rules. Shipping a useful backend layer and deferring an unsafe database cutover can be the right engineering decision.
