## The brief

Our hackathon team explored how to turn a client brief into a sales proposal. In 24 hours, we had to make the idea understandable to someone seeing it for the first time.

## The design

A proposal combines several kinds of work. Someone needs to understand the requirements, describe a possible solution and estimate the effort. Our concept split those responsibilities across agents. The diagram is a reference design for that idea.

> **Trade-off.** Dividing the work makes each responsibility visible. It also creates handoffs. An estimate can look precise even if the original requirement was ambiguous.

## Team result

We placed second. The award was a team result for the hackathon project.

## After the hackathon

The prototype outlived the weekend. A separate team later adopted it into the GeekyAnts website.
